Little Lasts

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Little Lasts does not collect any data about you.

There is no account to create and no server run by the developer. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores

The things you keep track of, the times you log, your notes and photos, the people you add and their birthdays are saved in the app's own database on your iPhone.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps your records in sync across your own devices through Apple's iCloud, in your private iCloud database. Only your Apple Account can read it; the developer cannot see it. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can turn it off in Settings → your name → iCloud.

If you sign out of iCloud, the app removes its copy from that iPhone; your records stay in your iCloud and come back when you sign in again.

Photos

You choose a photo with the system photo picker. The app receives only the photo you picked and has no access to the rest of your library.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone itself. No server sends them and nothing about them leaves your device.

What happens to your data

  • It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • It is not shared or sold.
  • It is not used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry, thread or person inside the app. Deleting the app removes what it stored on your iPhone. To also remove the copy in iCloud, open Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Little Lasts and delete its data.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top.

Contact

应用保存了什么

你记录的事、每一次的日期、写下的话和照片、添加的人和他们的生日，都保存在 你 iPhone 上属于本应用的数据库里。

iCloud

如果你登录了 iCloud，应用会通过 Apple 的 iCloud，在你自己的设备之间同步这些 记录。数据存放在你的 iCloud 私有数据库里，只有你的 Apple 账户能读取，开发者 看不到。这部分由 Apple 依照其隐私政策处理。你可以在「设置 → 你的名字 → iCloud」中关闭。

如果你退出 iCloud，应用会删除这台 iPhone 上的副本；记录仍保存在你的 iCloud 中，重新登录后会回来。

照片

照片通过系统的照片选择器挑选。应用只拿到你选中的那一张，无法访问相册里的 其他内容。

通知

提醒由你的 iPhone 自己安排，不经过任何服务器，相关信息不会离开你的设备。

你的数据会被如何处理

  • 不会发送给开发者或任何人。
  • 不会被共享或出售。
  • 不会用于广告或数据分析。

删除你的数据

任何一笔记录、一条线或一个人，都可以在应用里删除。删除应用会清除它在 iPhone 上保存的内容。若要同时删除 iCloud 中的副本，请打开「设置 → 你的名字 → iCloud → 管理账户储存空间 → 最后一次」，删除其数据。

儿童

本应用不向任何人收集数据，儿童也不例外。

政策变更

如果本政策有任何变更，更新后的版本会发布在此地址，并在顶部标注新的日期。
