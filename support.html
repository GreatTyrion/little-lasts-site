How does it work?

Add something that keeps happening — a bedtime story, a weekly call, a dish someone makes — and log it each time it happens, in a few seconds. Each thread learns its own rhythm. When one goes quiet for much longer than usual, the app gently asks whether that was the last time. Say yes and it keeps that last time as a memory.

Why hasn't it asked me anything yet?

It only asks once a thread has been quiet for about three times its usual gap, and never sooner than two weeks. A new thread also waits a day, so you can log older times without being asked straight away.

How do I add a person?

Open Settings, then tap Add a person under People. You can also add one while creating a thread, under Person.

What does “In memory” do?

Tap a person's name on the home screen to edit them. With In memory on, the app stops asking whether things with them will happen again, and keeps their threads together at the bottom of the home screen.

Does it sync between my devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud, when you are signed in with the same Apple Account. Without iCloud everything stays on this iPhone.

How do notifications work?

At most one a day, at the time you choose in Settings. The gentle notes on holidays and birthdays are off until you turn them on.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your iPhone. To change it for this app only, open Settings → Apps → Little Lasts → Language.

它是怎么用的？

把一件反复发生的事记下来——一个睡前故事、每周一通电话、某个人做的一道菜—— 每次发生时花几秒记一笔。每条线会摸清自己的节奏。当它安静得比平时久很多时， 应用会轻轻问一句：那会是最后一次吗？回答「是」，它就替你记住那最后一次。

为什么它还没问过我？

一条线要安静到平时间隔的大约三倍才会问，而且至少要两周。新建的线还会等一天， 方便你先补记以前的几次，不会马上被问。

怎么添加一个人？

打开「设置」，在「人」下面点新建一个人。新建一件事时，也可以在 这件事关于谁里添加。

「怀念 TA」是什么意思？

在首页点一个人的名字即可编辑。打开怀念 TA后，应用不会再问和 TA 有关的事是否还会发生，TA 的线会放在首页底部的「怀念」里。

会在我的设备之间同步吗？

会，通过你自己的 iCloud，只要各设备登录同一个 Apple 账户。没有 iCloud 时， 所有内容都只保存在这台 iPhone 上。

通知是怎样的？

每天最多一条，时间在「设置」里选。节日和生日的轻轻提醒默认关闭，需要时再打开。

怎么切换语言？

应用跟随 iPhone 的语言设置。若只想改这一个应用，请打开「设置 → App → 最后一次 → 语言」。
